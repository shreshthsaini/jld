# Example image provenance

The reference photographs come from the Kodak lossless image suite hosted by
Rich Franzen at https://r0k.us/graphics/kodak/. That page states his understanding
that Eastman Kodak released the images for unrestricted usage. Consult the
original source for the image reuse statement; the photographs are separate
from the MIT license covering JLD's code.

`make_examples.py` selects three photographs and creates the released JPEG,
blur, noise, and color-shift examples. `expected.json` records their JLD scores.
The images demonstrate the metric and are not a new benchmark evaluation.
